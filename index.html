Buckhead Notary and Apostille Mobile Services, a trade name of Buckhead Regulatory Compliance Partners, LLC, was founded on the belief that Atlanta's organizations and individuals deserve more than just a signature — they deserve a trusted partner who brings unmatched expertise, discretion, and professionalism to every engagement.

Led by Dr. Lonie Stanford Haynes — holding a D.L.P., M.P.A., M.Sc., PMP® certification and the SHRM AI+HI Specialty Credential — our firm combines 30+ years of business and law experience, including 20+ years of executive leadership, with the discipline and precision of a U.S. Army veteran. Together, Dr. Haynes and our team bring more than 60 years of combined business experience across the corporate, government, academic and non-profit sectors, including healthcare, education, communications and the military.

Dr. Haynes's career has always crossed borders. He served with the U.S. Army in West Germany during the Cold War, then returned to Germany with AT&T, working in Munich, Hamburg and Frankfurt and traveling on business to France, England, the Netherlands and across Scandinavia. With AT&T, Sprint and AmerisourceBergen, where he served as Global Chief Diversity & Inclusion Officer, he led work spanning Asia-Pacific, Europe, the Middle East and Africa, and South America. Today, as founding partner of RD – Raison d’Être Consultancy Group, he advises clients in the United Kingdom, Singapore and the Middle East, so he knows firsthand how documents, institutions and expectations differ from one country to the next.

As a former adjunct professor at Georgetown University’s McCourt School of Public Policy, with a doctorate in law and policy from Northeastern University, a master of science in management from Boston University, and a master of public administration from the City University of New York’s Baruch College, Dr. Haynes has a firm understanding of the policy behind cross-border documents: the Hague Apostille Convention, embassy and consular legalization, and the rules governments use to decide which foreign records they will accept. That perspective helps clients get each requirement right the first time.

We specialize in mobile notary services, apostille authentication, embassy legalization, and complex document signings for corporations, healthcare organizations, universities and law firms across Buckhead, Midtown, Cumberland, Vinings and Emory–Druid Hills.

We understand that cost, convenience, and trust are paramount when handling your most sensitive documents. That's why we come to you — at your home, office, hospital, or any location — with Saturday appointments and phone lines open 8 a.m. to 10 p.m., seven days a week.

Apostille · Authentication · Legalization

Apostille and Authentication & Legalization Services

Whatever country your documents are going to, we get them accepted. Countries that belong to the Hague Apostille Convention accept an apostille, a single certificate that authenticates your document. Countries outside the Convention require authentication and embassy legalization instead. We handle both, step by step, whether your document originated in Georgia or elsewhere.

Apostille Countries

Hague Convention members, for example China, India, Mexico, the United Kingdom and most of Europe.

Legalization Countries

Non-Hague countries, for example Nigeria, Ghana, the United Arab Emirates, Egypt and the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Country requirements change. We confirm the current rules for your destination before every order.

1

Verify Your Destination Country

We confirm whether your destination country is a Hague Apostille member, or requires a different legalization process.

2

Prepare Your Document

We review your original document — birth certificate, diploma, legal record, or corporate filing — to ensure it is ready for authentication.

3

Submit to the GSCCCA

In Georgia, apostilles are issued by the Georgia Superior Court Clerks' Cooperative Authority (GSCCCA). We hand-deliver your documents, with same-day expedited processing available.

4

Apostille or Embassy Legalization

For Hague countries, your apostilled document is ready to use. For non-Hague countries, we continue through the U.S. Department of State and the destination country's embassy or consulate.

Note: We can assist with apostille services even if your document was issued outside of Georgia. Contact us for a consultation and we'll guide you through the process step by step.

Authentication & Embassy Legalization

For Countries That Don't Accept an Apostille

An apostille only works in countries that belong to the Hague Apostille Convention. Many countries in Africa, the Middle East and Asia are not members, so their documents need authentication and embassy legalization instead: a longer chain of certifications from the state, often the U.S. Department of State, and finally the destination country's embassy or consulate.

Atlanta is home to a large international community and one of the largest consular corps in the country. We handle the entire chain for you, personally and locally, with white-glove care from the first review to hand delivery of the finished documents.

1

Confirm the Country's Requirements

We check whether your destination accepts an apostille or requires full legalization, and what that embassy or consulate expects.

2

Prepare and Notarize

We review each document, arrange certified copies where needed, and complete notarization and supporting affidavits.

3

State and Federal Certification

We submit to the Georgia authority (GSCCCA) and, when required, the U.S. Department of State. Federal documents such as FBI background checks go directly to the State Department.

4

Embassy or Consulate Legalization

We deliver your documents to the embassy or consulate for its final seal, then return them to you by hand.
